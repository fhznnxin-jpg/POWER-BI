2. Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Music Analysis

To analyse the audio characteristics of popular songs, including:

Danceability
Energy
Acousticness
Instrumentalness
Liveness
Speechiness
Valence
2. Platform Comparison

To compare the presence of songs across:

Spotify
Apple Music
Deezer
Shazam
3. Artist Impact

To analyse whether songs involving different numbers of artists show differences in streaming performance.

4. Temporal Trends

To study how streaming performance and audio characteristics vary according to the release year.

5. Cross-Platform Presence

To examine how songs represented in the dataset appear across different music platforms.
