Aim
To integrate advanced analytics with Business Intelligence reports using Microsoft Power BI
and generate business insights, strategic recommendations, and executive presentations that
support organizational decision-making.
Tools / Software Required
1. Microsoft Power BI Desktop
2. Global Superstore Dataset.xlsx
3. Power Query Editor
4. Microsoft PowerPoint
Dataset Source:
Global Superstore Dataset (Kaggle)
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/apoorvaappz/global-super-store-dataset
Theory
Advanced Analytics in Business Intelligence focuses on transforming data into actionable
insights. While traditional BI reports present information, advanced analytics helps identify
trends, opportunities, business challenges, and improvement areas. Organizations use analytical
reports and dashboards to support strategic planning and decision-making.
Power BI enables advanced analytics through KPI analysis, trend analysis, comparative analysis,
ranking, segmentation, and interactive reporting. The ultimate objective is to convert analytical
findings into meaningful business recommendations.
Business Scenario
Global Superstore management has observed that sales are increasing but profitability is not
growing at the expected rate. Senior management requires an analytical review of business
performance. Students will act as Business Intelligence Consultants and use Power BI reports
and dashboards to identify issues, discover opportunities, and recommend corrective actions.
Conduct - Part A: Analyze Existing Reports and Dashboards
Step 1: Open the reports and dashboards created in Experiments 5 and 6.
Step 2: Analyze Sales Performance by Region.
Step 3: Analyze Profitability by Product Category.
Step 4: Analyze Customer Segment Performance.
Step 5: Analyze Monthly and Yearly Sales Trends.
Step 6: Document significant observations.
Conduct - Part B: Business Problem Identification
Step 7: Identify at least two business strengths.
Step 8: Identify at least two business weaknesses.
Step 9: Identify at least two business opportunities.
Step 10: Support findings using charts, KPIs, and analytical evidence from Power BI.
Conduct - Part C: Business Insight Report Preparation
Step 11: Prepare a Business Insight Report.
The report should contain:
• Objective of Analysis
• Reports and Dashboards Used
• Key Findings
• Business Strengths
• Business Weaknesses
• Opportunities for Improvement
• Recommendations
• Conclusion
Step 12: Include screenshots from Power BI reports and dashboards.
Conduct - Part D: Executive Presentation
Step 13: Prepare a presentation consisting of 5–7 slides.
Suggested Structure:
• Introduction
• Dashboard Overview
• Key Findings
• Business Issues Identified
• Recommendations
• Conclusion
Step 14: Present findings before the instructor or class.
Step 15: Justify recommendations using data-driven evidence.
Deliverables
1. Business Insight Report (PDF/Word)
2. Executive Presentation (PPT/PDF)
3. Recommendation Summary Sheet
4. Supporting Power BI Dashboard (.pbix)
Result
Advanced analytics techniques were successfully integrated with Business Intelligence reports
and dashboards. Business strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and recommendations were
identified and communicated through a professional report and executive presentation.

• Generate business insights from analytical findings. (CO5-BT6)
• Develop strategic recommendations based on data-driven evidence. (CO5-BT6)
• Communicate analytical findings through professional reports and presentations. (CO5-BT6)
